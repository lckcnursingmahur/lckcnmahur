ABOUT US

Welcome to Our Nursing College

स्व. चंद्रभागाबाई केशवे कॉलेज ऑफ नर्सिंग

Late Chandrabhagabai Keshave College of Nursing, Mahur, provides professional education and practical training for students who wish to build a rewarding career in nursing.

Our GNM programme focuses on developing knowledgeable, skilled, compassionate and responsible nursing professionals.

Students receive academic guidance together with practical clinical experience to prepare them for different healthcare settings.
