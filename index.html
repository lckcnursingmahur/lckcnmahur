ABOUT US

Welcome to Late Chandrabhagabai Keshave College of Nursing

Late Chandrabhagabai Keshave College of Nursing (GNM), Mahur, is dedicated to providing quality nursing education and preparing students for responsible and compassionate professional practice.

Our focus is on academic knowledge, practical nursing skills, professional values, discipline and patient-centred care.
